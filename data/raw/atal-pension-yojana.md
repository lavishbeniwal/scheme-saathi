# Atal Pension Yojana

## Overview
Atal Pension Yojana (APY) is a government-backed pension scheme introduced in 2015 to provide a universal social security system for Indians, especially the poor, under-privileged, and workers in the unorganised sector. The scheme is administered by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) under the overall administrative and institutional architecture of the National Pension System (NPS), and it operates through public and private banks, regional rural banks, small finance banks, payment banks, and postal services.

## Eligibility
- Open to all citizens of India.
- Minimum age 18 years and maximum age 40 years at the time of enrolment.
- Must hold (or open) a savings bank account or post office savings bank account.
- As of 1 October 2022, any individual who is or has been an income-tax payer is not eligible to join APY.

## Benefits
- Central Government guaranteed minimum monthly pension after age 60, at one of five slabs: ₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹3,000, ₹4,000, or ₹5,000 per month, based on the subscriber's contribution.
- Spousal pension: on the subscriber's death, the spouse receives the same pension amount until their own death.
- Wealth transfer: after both the subscriber and spouse have passed away, the nominee receives the accumulated pension corpus.
- Monthly contributions are collected via auto-debit from the subscriber's linked savings account from enrolment until age 60.

## Required Documents
- Active savings bank account or post office savings account with auto-debit facility enabled.
- Aadhaar enrolment number, or Aadhaar-based authentication, is mandatory.
- APY Registration Form (available at banks/post offices, or online via eAPY).
