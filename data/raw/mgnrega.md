# MGNREGA (Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act)

## Overview
The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA), 2005 is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India. It is a demand-driven wage employment scheme, meaning eligible households can demand work when they need it, and the Ministry seeks additional funds as required to meet the demand for work on the ground.

## Eligibility
- Every adult member of a household in a rural area, willing to do unskilled manual work and holding a job card issued under the Mahatma Gandhi NREG Act, is eligible to demand work under the scheme.

## Benefits
- The Act is designed to provide at least 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to every rural household whose adult member volunteers to do unskilled manual work.
- An additional 50 days of wage employment (over and above the 100 days) is provided in notified drought-affected areas or natural calamity areas.
- Wages are paid at rates notified by the Central Government; the Ministry of Rural Development notifies MGNREGA wage rates every year for each State/UT, revised based on changes in the Consumer Price Index for Agricultural Labour (CPI-AL), applicable from 1st April of each financial year.
- As of recent reporting, 99.7% of MGNREGA wage payments are made through e-transfer (direct bank/post office transfer).

## Required Documents
- A job card, issued under the Mahatma Gandhi NREG Act, is required to demand work under the scheme.
- Not specified on official source; verify separately for the full list of documents needed to apply for a job card (e.g., proof of residence, photographs).
