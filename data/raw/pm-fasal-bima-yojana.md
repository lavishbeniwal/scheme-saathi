# PM Fasal Bima Yojana

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) is the Government of India's crop insurance scheme, implemented by the Department of Agriculture, Cooperation and Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare. It provides insurance coverage and financial support to farmers in the event of failure of notified crops due to natural calamities, pests, and diseases, with the objective of stabilising farmers' income.

## Eligibility
- All farmers growing notified crops in notified areas during a season are eligible for coverage, including sharecroppers and tenant farmers, provided they have an insurable interest in the notified/insured crop.
- Loanee farmers: all farmers/Kisan Credit Card holders who have availed seasonal agricultural loans for notified crops in a notified reference unit area are covered.
- Non-loanee farmers: any farmer willing to avail crop insurance cover for notified crops in a notified reference unit area can enrol voluntarily.

## Benefits
- Uniform premium payable by farmers: 2% of the sum insured for Kharif crops, 1.5% for Rabi crops, and 5% for annual commercial and horticultural crops.
- The balance of the actuarial premium (beyond the farmer's share) is subsidised and paid by the Government, so farmers receive the full insured amount against crop loss from natural calamities.
- Claim payouts are made via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the farmer's linked bank account.

## Required Documents
- Aadhaar number (mandatory for availing crop insurance under PMFBY since the Kharif 2017 season).
- Bank account details (passbook/cancelled cheque) for DBT of claim amounts.
- Declaration about the crop sown, submitted along with the application.
- Land records and other enrolment documents as specified by the concerned state government/insurance company; not fully specified on official source, verify separately for state-specific requirements.
