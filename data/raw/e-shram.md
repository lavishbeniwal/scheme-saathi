# e-Shram

## Overview
e-Shram is the National Database of Unorganised Workers (NDUW), launched in August 2021 by the Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India. It registers unorganised sector workers (aged 16–59) — including home-based, self-employed, and wage workers who are not members of ESIC or EPFO — and issues each worker a permanent Universal Account Number (UAN) via an e-Shram card. The portal is intended to facilitate delivery of social security and welfare schemes of the Central and State Governments to unorganised workers, including migrant, gig, agricultural, construction, and landless farm workers.

## Eligibility
- Any home-based worker, self-employed worker, or wage worker in the unorganised sector who is not a member of ESIC or EPFO.
- Age 16 years and above (up to 59 years) engaged in unorganised work.
- No income criteria for registration; however, the worker should not be an income tax payee.
- Agricultural labourers and landless farmers also qualify.

## Benefits
- Access to government social security and welfare schemes mapped/integrated with e-Shram, including (among others):
  - Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-Dhan Yojana (PM-SYM) — monthly pension of ₹3,000 after age 60 for unorganised workers aged 18–40 with monthly income below ₹15,000.
  - Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) — ₹2 lakh life cover on death due to any cause, annual premium ₹436.
  - Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) — ₹2 lakh for accidental death/full disability and ₹1 lakh for partial disability, annual premium ₹20.
  - Ayushman Bharat (AB-PMJAY) — health coverage of ₹5 lakh per family per year.
  - Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G) — housing assistance of ₹1.2–1.3 lakh for eligible families.
  - National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP) — monthly pensions ranging from ₹1,000–₹3,000 for eligible elderly persons.
- Permanent Universal Account Number (UAN) with non-expiring card validity.
- Enhanced portability of social security benefits, particularly for migrant and construction workers.
- Potential emergency/ad-hoc assistance during national crises.
- Note: as of official information, direct financial benefits are not yet disbursed through e-Shram itself — registration primarily enables eligibility for other linked schemes.

## Required Documents
- Aadhaar number.
- Aadhaar-linked mobile number (for OTP-based self-registration).
- No other documents/certificates (e.g., proof of date of birth or income) are required at Common Service Centres (CSCs).
- Workers without an Aadhaar-linked mobile number can register via biometric authentication at a CSC or State Seva Kendra (SSK).
- Registration is free of charge.
