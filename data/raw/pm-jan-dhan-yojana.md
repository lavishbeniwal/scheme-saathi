# PM Jan Dhan Yojana

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) is described by the government as "A Revolutionary Financial Inclusion Program" aimed at expanding banking access across India. It is managed by the Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India, through a dedicated PMJDY Mission Office. As of the fetched data, 59.09 crore beneficiaries have been enrolled, holding a total balance of ₹316,513.80 crore, supported by 13.55 lakh Bank Mitras providing branchless banking services nationwide.

## Eligibility
- A Basic Savings Bank Deposit (BSBD) account under PMJDY can be opened by any person who does not already have another bank account.
- Accounts can be opened at any bank branch or at a Business Correspondent (Bank Mitra) outlet.

## Benefits
- No requirement to maintain any minimum balance in PMJDY accounts.
- Deposits in the account earn interest.
- Free RuPay debit card provided to account holders.
- Accidental insurance cover under PMJDY: ₹2 lakh for accounts opened after 28 August 2018, and ₹1 lakh for accounts opened earlier.
- Overdraft (OD) facility of up to ₹10,000 available to eligible account holders.
- Access/linkage to Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT), Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana, Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana, Atal Pension Yojana, and the MUDRA scheme.

## Required Documents
- Aadhaar card (primary KYC document for opening the account).
- Fresh KYC documents must be submitted on attaining majority (18 years) in the case of accounts opened for minors.
- Not specified on official source; verify separately for the full list of accepted Officially Valid Documents (OVDs) when Aadhaar is unavailable.
