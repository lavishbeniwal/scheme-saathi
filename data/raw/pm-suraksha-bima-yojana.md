# PM Suraksha Bima Yojana

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) is a one-year, renewable Personal Accident Insurance Scheme offered by the Government of India, administered by the Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance. It is offered/administered through Public Sector General Insurance Companies (PSGICs) and other willing general insurance companies, in collaboration with participating banks, and provides protection against death or disability caused by accident.

## Eligibility
- Individual bank/post office account holders (single or joint) aged between 18 and 70 years.
- Must give consent to join and enable auto-debit for the premium, on or before 31 May each year, for the coverage period 1 June to 31 May (annual renewal basis).
- A person can enrol through only one account, even if they hold multiple bank accounts.
- All bank account holders other than institutional account holders are eligible.
- NRIs with an eligible bank account at a branch located in India are also eligible, subject to the scheme's terms and conditions.

## Benefits
- Annual premium of ₹20 per member, auto-debited from the subscriber's bank/post office account in one instalment.
- Coverage of ₹2 lakh for accidental death or total and irrecoverable loss of both eyes/both hands/both feet or loss of one eye and one hand/foot.
- Coverage of ₹1 lakh for total and irrecoverable loss of one eye or one limb (partial disability).
- Natural calamities are treated as accidents, so death/disability resulting from such events is also covered.
- Death due to suicide is excluded from coverage; death due to murder is covered.

## Required Documents
- PMSBY enrolment form (prescribed format).
- Auto-debit authorisation for premium deduction from the linked bank/post office account.
- Consent and declaration form.
- Valid identification and bank account details.
