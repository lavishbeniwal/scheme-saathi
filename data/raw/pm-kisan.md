# PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

## Overview
PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) is a Central Sector scheme with 100% funding from the Government of India. It became operational from 1.12.2018. The scheme operates under the Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India. State governments and UT administrations are responsible for identifying eligible farmer families.

## Eligibility
- The scheme's definition of a "family" is husband, wife, and minor children.
- State governments/UT administrations identify eligible farmer families based on scheme guidelines.
- The following categories are excluded (ineligible) from the scheme:
  - Institutional landholders
  - Government officials and pensioners earning ₹10,000+ per month
  - Those who paid income tax in the last assessment year
  - Constitutional office holders and certain categories of politicians
  - Licensed professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants, etc.)
- Benefits have been temporarily withheld in suspected cases, including farmers who acquired land after February 1, 2019, and cases where multiple family members receive concurrent benefits.
- eKYC certification is mandatory for beneficiaries.

## Benefits
- Income support of ₹6,000 per year, paid in three equal installments of ₹2,000 each.
- Funds are transferred directly to beneficiaries' bank accounts (Direct Benefit Transfer).
- As of the most recent cycle, 23 installments have been released (the 23rd was released on June 20, 2026).

## Required Documents
- Details required for registration/enrollment via the official portal (self-registration, CSC, or state nodal officer).
- Bank account details (for Direct Benefit Transfer).
- eKYC certification (via OTP-based, biometric, or face authentication as available on the portal/app).
- Not specified on official source; verify separately for a full itemized document list (e.g. land records, Aadhaar).
