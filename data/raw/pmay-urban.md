# Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U) is a credit-linked subsidy scheme by the Government of India, implemented by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, to facilitate access to affordable housing for low- and moderate-income residents of the country ("Housing for All").

## Eligibility
- The applicant should not have availed any central/state government housing subsidy or benefit under the PMAY scheme previously.
- The applicant should not own a pucca house, either in their own name or in the name of any family member (including dependents), anywhere in India.
- In the case of a married couple, either spouse individually, or both jointly, will be eligible for a single house, subject to the household's income eligibility under the scheme.

## Benefits
PMAY-Urban operates through four verticals:
- Beneficiary-led Individual House Construction or Enhancement (BLC): Central Assistance of ₹1.5 lakh to individual eligible families in the EWS category.
- Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP): Central Assistance of ₹1.5 lakh per EWS house in projects where at least 35% of houses are for the EWS category and the project has at least 250 houses.
- In-situ Slum Redevelopment (ISSR): Slum redevelopment grant of ₹1 lakh per house for eligible slum dwellers.
- Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS): Interest subsidy of 6.5% for Economically Weaker Section (EWS) and Low Income Group (LIG) beneficiaries on housing loans, for a tenure of 20 years or the loan tenure, whichever is lower.

## Required Documents
- Passport-size photograph of the applicant
- Photograph of the house with the applicant
- Aadhaar card
- Ration card
- Voter ID
- Proof of ownership (of land/plot, where applicable)
- Photocopy of bank account passbook
- Affidavit regarding proper utilization of the grant
- Income declaration
