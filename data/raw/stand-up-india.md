# Stand-Up India Scheme

## Overview
The Stand-Up India Scheme facilitates bank loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore to at least one Scheduled Caste (SC)/Scheduled Tribe (ST) borrower and at least one woman borrower per bank branch, for setting up a new (greenfield) enterprise. The scheme is administered through participating scheduled commercial bank branches (73+ lenders onboarded), supported by handholding agencies, Lead District Managers (LDMs), and the official Stand-Up India portal (standupmitra.in).

## Eligibility
- SC/ST and/or women entrepreneurs above 18 years of age, setting up a new (greenfield) enterprise.
- At least one SC/ST borrower and at least one woman borrower per bank branch.
- For non-individual enterprises, at least 51% of the shareholding and controlling stake must be held by an SC/ST or woman entrepreneur.
- Eligible sectors: manufacturing, services, agri-allied activities, and trading.
- Borrower should not be in default with any bank or financial institution.

## Benefits
- Composite loans (term loan plus working capital) ranging from ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore.
- Loan expected to cover up to 85% of the project cost (where the borrower's own contribution, along with convergence support from other schemes, does not meet 15% of project cost).
- Margin money requirement: 15% of project cost, of which the borrower must contribute a minimum of 10% from their own resources.
- Interest rate: lowest applicable rate of the bank for that category (rating), not exceeding base rate (MCLR) + 3% + tenor premium.
- Repayment period up to 7 years, with a moratorium of up to 18 months.
- Credit Guarantee Fund Scheme for Stand-Up India Loans (CGFSIL) provides security coverage.
- A RuPay card is provided to enable working capital access.
- Handholding support: training, project report preparation assistance, and identification of infrastructure/workspace.

## Required Documents
- Proof of identity: Voter's ID Card, Passport, Driving License, PAN Card, or signature identification from the applicant's current banker.
- Proof of residence: recent telephone/electricity bill, property tax receipt, Passport, or Voter's ID Card.
- Proof of business address.
- Caste certificate (for SC/ST category applicants).
- Detailed Project Report / business plan for the proposed greenfield enterprise.
- KYC documents, uploaded through the Stand-Up India portal for bank appraisal.
- (Official guidelines note that exact document lists/checklists vary by loan category and are available as downloadable checklists on the portal, e.g., for Shishu, Kishore, Tarun, and Stand-Up loan categories.)
