# National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP)

## Overview
The National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP), introduced on 15 August 1995, is a fully funded Centrally Sponsored Scheme implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India. It extends financial support (social assistance/pensions) to elderly persons, widows, and persons with disabilities living below the poverty line (BPL), across both rural and urban areas. NSAP covers old age pension, widow pension, disability pension, family benefit on the death of a breadwinner, and food security assistance (Annapurna). As of recent figures, NSAP caters to around 3.09 crore beneficiaries, with a scheme-wise ceiling/cap on the number of beneficiaries for each State/UT.

## Eligibility
- Applicant must generally belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) household, as per criteria prescribed by the Government of India.
- **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS):** BPL persons aged 60 years or above.
- **Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme (IGNWPS):** Widows in the age group of 40–59 years (some sources cite 40–79 years) belonging to a BPL household.
- **Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme (IGNDPS):** Persons aged 18–59 years (some sources cite 18–79 years) with severe and multiple disabilities, belonging to a BPL household.
- **National Family Benefit Scheme (NFBS):** BPL household on the death of the primary breadwinner aged between 18 and 59/64 years (varies by source).
- **Annapurna Scheme:** Senior citizens who are eligible for old age pension but remain uncovered under the old age pension scheme.

## Benefits
- **Old Age Pension (IGNOAPS):** Central pension of ₹200/month per beneficiary from age 60 to 79, and ₹500/month from age 80 onwards. States/UTs are encouraged to add a top-up of at least an equivalent amount; actual top-ups range from ₹50 to ₹3,800/month, bringing the average monthly pension in most States/UTs to around ₹1,000.
- **Widow Pension (IGNWPS):** ₹300/month per beneficiary.
- **Disability Pension (IGNDPS):** ₹300/month per beneficiary.
- **National Family Benefit Scheme (NFBS):** One-time lump sum of ₹20,000 to the bereaved family on death of the primary breadwinner.
- **Annapurna Scheme:** 10 kg of food grains per month, free of cost, to eligible uncovered senior citizens.
