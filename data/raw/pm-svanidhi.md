# PM SVANidhi (PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi)

## Overview
PM SVANidhi (Pradhan Mantri Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi) is a micro-credit scheme for street vendors, run under the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), with SIDBI and the Department of Financial Services supporting implementation. Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) and Town Vending Committees (TVCs) conduct vendor surveys and process eligibility documentation. The scheme provides collateral-free working capital loans to street vendors to help them resume and grow their livelihoods.

## Eligibility
- Street vendors recognised under the Street Vendors (Protection of Livelihood and Regulation of Street Vending) Act, 2014, who were engaged in vending prior to 24 March 2020, as identified through ULB surveys.
- Vendors from Census Towns are also eligible.
- Applicants must possess a Certificate of Vending or a Letter of Recommendation (LoR) issued by the ULB or Town Vending Committee.
- Available only to beneficiaries in States/UTs that have notified rules and a scheme under the Street Vendors Act, 2014.
- Mobile number must be linked with Aadhaar.

## Benefits
- Tiered, collateral-free working capital loans:
  - First loan: up to ₹10,000 (portal references also cite a ₹15,000 tier in later documentation)
  - Second loan: up to ₹20,000 (also referenced as ₹25,000 in later documentation)
  - Third loan: up to ₹50,000
- RuPay/credit card facility available for vendors who have repaid earlier tranches, to help increase working capital.
- Applications, e-KYC, and monitoring can be done through the mobile app or web portal, with support from Banking Correspondents/Agents.

## Required Documents
- Certificate of Vending (CoV) or ID card issued by the ULB, or a Letter of Recommendation (LoR) from the ULB/Town Vending Committee.
- Aadhaar number, with mobile number linked to Aadhaar (for e-KYC).
- Bank account details.
- Loan Application Form (LAF), available on the PM SVANidhi portal.
