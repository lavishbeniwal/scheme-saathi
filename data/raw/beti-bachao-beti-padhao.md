# Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP)

## Overview
Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP) is a mindset-change programme launched by the Prime Minister on 22 January 2015 at Panipat, Haryana, aimed at generating awareness for valuing the girl child through multi-sectoral interventions. It is a tri-ministerial effort involving the Ministries of Women and Child Development, Health & Family Welfare, and (erstwhile) Human Resource Development, with the Ministry of Women and Child Development as the nodal ministry. The scheme focuses on ground-level awareness and behavioural-change activities rather than direct financial transfers.

## Eligibility
- Not a direct-benefit scheme with individual applicant eligibility criteria.
- Primary target groups: newly married couples, expecting/new parents, adolescents, and communities.
- Secondary target groups: schools, medical practitioners, hospitals, government officials, Panchayati Raj Institutions/Urban Local Bodies, frontline workers, civil society organisations, and religious leaders.

## Benefits
- No provision for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) or creation of individual capital assets under this scheme.
- The programme delivers benefits through awareness generation, advocacy, and multi-sectoral action aimed at:
  - Preventing gender-biased sex-selective practices.
  - Ensuring survival, protection, education, and development of the girl child.
  - Improving the sex ratio at birth.
  - Improving institutional deliveries and ante-natal care registration.
  - Increasing secondary education enrollment for girls and reducing dropout rates.
  - Promoting awareness of safe menstrual hygiene and management.
- Provision of operational manuals (in 11 regional languages), a compendium of best practices, and pledge/advocacy material to implementing stakeholders.
