# Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (formerly Indira Awaas Yojana) is a social welfare programme under the Ministry of Rural Development, Government of India, to provide pucca houses with basic amenities to the rural poor in India. The Ministry of Rural Development has been implementing PMAY-G with effect from 1st April 2016, with an overall target to construct 2.95 crore pucca houses with basic amenities by March 2024.

## Eligibility
- Beneficiaries are identified based on the housing deprivation parameters prescribed under the Socio Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011.
- No application from households is required under PMAY-G; beneficiaries are identified from the Permanent Wait List (PWL) of PMAY-G, based on SECC 2011 and the finalized Awaas+ list.
- Households having a total land holding of 7.5 acres or more (irrigated or non-irrigated) are excluded from PMAY-G.

## Benefits
- Financial assistance to eligible rural households for construction of pucca houses with basic amenities.
- Not specified on official source; verify separately for the exact per-house assistance amount and plain-area/hilly-area differentiation.

## Required Documents
- No separate application or document submission is required from households to be included, since beneficiaries are identified directly from the SECC 2011-based Permanent Wait List (PWL) and the Awaas+ survey.
- Not specified on official source; verify separately for documents needed at the disbursal/verification stage (e.g., Aadhaar-linked bank account for installment transfer).
