# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana

## Overview
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (Sukanya Samriddhi Account, SSA) is a small savings scheme of the Government of India specifically designed for the welfare of the girl child, aimed at encouraging parents/guardians to build a fund for a girl child's future education and marriage expenses. The scheme falls under the Ministry of Finance's Small Savings Schemes and accounts can be opened at India Post offices or at authorised bank branches.

## Eligibility
- Account can be opened in the name of a girl child from birth until she attains the age of 10 years.
- Only a parent or legal guardian of the girl child can open and operate the account on her behalf.
- The account must be opened for an Indian girl child.
- Accounts can be opened at any India Post office, any branch of a Public Sector Bank, or authorised Private Sector Banks (e.g., HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, ICICI Bank, IDBI Bank).

## Benefits
- Offers the highest interest rate among Small Savings Schemes; the rate is periodically notified by the Ministry of Finance (7.6% per annum as per the source referenced below).
- Minimum initial/annual deposit of ₹250, with subsequent deposits allowed in multiples of ₹50; at least ₹250 must be deposited in a financial year to keep the account active.
- Maximum annual deposit limit of ₹1,50,000 per account.
- Long-term, government-backed savings instrument intended to fund a girl child's higher education and marriage expenses.

## Required Documents
- SSA account opening application form.
- Birth certificate of the girl child.
- Identity proof of the parent/guardian opening the account.
