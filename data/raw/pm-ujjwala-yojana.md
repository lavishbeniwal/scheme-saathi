# Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) is a government scheme providing LPG (cooking gas) connections to eligible women from poor households. It is administered by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas (MOPNG) in partnership with three Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs): Indian Oil (Indane), BPCL (Bharat Gas), and HPCL (HP Gas). As of August 26, 2026, the program has released over 105.6 million connections, with 2.4 million released under the latest extension (PMUY 2.0).

## Eligibility
- The applicant must be a woman who has attained 18 years of age.
- There should be no other LPG connection from any Oil Marketing Company (OMC) within the same household.
- The adult woman must belong to a poor household, based on submission of a deprivation declaration.

## Benefits
- Cash assistance of ₹2,050 for 14.2 kg cylinder connections, or ₹1,300 for 5 kg cylinder connections, covering deposit, regulator, hose, card, and installation charges.
- All PMUY beneficiaries are provided with a first LPG refill and a stove (hotplate), both free of cost.

## Required Documents
- KYC form
- Aadhaar copy
- Proof of address (if needed)
- Ration card or other government document confirming family composition
- Bank account details
- Deprivation declaration
