# Ayushman Bharat / PM-JAY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana)

## Overview
Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB PM-JAY) is described by the National Health Authority as "the largest health assurance scheme in the world," aiming to provide a health cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization. The scheme is administered by the National Health Authority (NHA), Government of India.

## Eligibility
- The scheme aims to provide health cover to over 12 crore poor and vulnerable families (approximately 55 crore beneficiaries), forming the bottom 40% of the Indian population.
- Households are included based on the deprivation and occupational criteria of the Socio-Economic Caste Census 2011 (SECC 2011), applied separately for rural and urban areas.
- The scheme was later expanded to include all citizens aged 70 years and above, regardless of their economic status.

## Benefits
- Health cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.
- Free secondary health care for those needing specialist treatment and free tertiary health care for those requiring hospitalization.
- Coverage for secondary, tertiary, and day-care procedures (as applicable) for treatment of diseases and medical conditions.
- Cashless treatment through a network of Empaneled Health Care Providers (EHCP).

## Required Documents
- Aadhaar is the primary document required for enrollment and for eKYC (Electronic Know Your Customer) verification against the UIDAI database.
- Not specified on official source; verify separately for a complete itemized document checklist (e.g., ration card, income/caste certificates) beyond Aadhaar.
