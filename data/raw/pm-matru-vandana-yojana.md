# Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY) is a maternity benefit scheme owned and run by the Ministry of Women and Child Development, Government of India. It provides financial support (partial wage-loss compensation) to pregnant women and lactating mothers for the birth of their child, along with supporting nutrition and health during pregnancy. The scheme is implemented on the ground by field functionaries such as Anganwadi Workers (AWWs), ASHA workers, and Lady Supervisors/ANMs, and beneficiaries can also register and track their claims through the PMMVYsoft portal and mobile app.

Payments are made via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the beneficiary's bank or post office account, which must be linked (seeded) with Aadhaar.

## Eligibility
- Pregnant women and lactating mothers, for the birth of their first living child.
- With effect from 1 April 2022, benefit is also extended for a second child if that child is a girl.
- Additional categories eligible for benefit regardless of other conditions include: women belonging to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, women who are partially (40%) or fully disabled (Divyang Jan), and women beneficiaries under Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) under Ayushman Bharat.
- Eligibility proof at registration includes women whose net family income is less than ₹8 lakh per annum, or women holding a MGNREGA Job Card.
- Beneficiary's bank/post office account must be Aadhaar-mapped/seeded for payment.

## Benefits
- For the first living child: ₹5,000 total, paid in two installments — ₹3,000 after registration and at least one Ante-Natal Check-up (ANC), and ₹2,000 after childbirth and completion of the child's first cycle of universal immunization (within 14 weeks of birth).
- For the second child, if a girl child (from 1 April 2022 onward): ₹6,000 paid as a single installment after childbirth and completion of universal immunization within 14 weeks.
- Benefits are disbursed directly into the Aadhaar-linked bank/post office account of the beneficiary via DBT.

## Required Documents
- Aadhaar card of the beneficiary.
- Aadhaar-mapped bank or post office account details.
- Mobile number.
- Eligibility proof (e.g., income certificate showing net family income below ₹8 lakh per annum, or MGNREGA Job Card).
- Mother and Child Protection (MCP) card / RCHI card.
- Details of Last Menstrual Period (LMP) date and Ante-Natal Check-up (ANC) date.
- Child birth certificate.
- Child immunization details.
- Application forms as applicable: Form 1-A (registration and first installment), Form 1-B (second installment claim), Form 1-C (third installment claim).
