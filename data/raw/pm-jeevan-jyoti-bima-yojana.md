# PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana

## Overview
Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY) is a Government of India one-year, renewable term life insurance scheme offering life cover for death due to any reason. It is administered by the Department of Financial Services through the Life Insurance Corporation (LIC) and other approved life insurers, in partnership with participating banks and post offices, under the Jan Suraksha umbrella of social security schemes.

## Eligibility
- Individuals aged between 18 and 50 years who hold a savings bank account or post office account with a participating bank are eligible to enrol.
- Aadhaar serves as the primary Know Your Customer (KYC) document for the scheme.
- A person can enrol through only one savings bank account, even if they hold multiple accounts across different banks.
- Members who join before completing 50 years of age can continue to have life risk covered up to age 55, subject to payment of regular premium.

## Benefits
- One-year renewable life insurance cover of ₹2 lakh, paid as a lump sum to the nominee if the insured member dies during the policy year.
- Premium is automatically debited from the subscriber's linked bank or post office account.
- Coverage is subject to scheme rules, including an initial lien/waiting period for non-accidental deaths in the first year of enrolment.

## Required Documents
- Consent-cum-Declaration Form / PMJJBY application form.
- Proof of identity (KYC) such as Aadhaar card, Electoral Photo Identity Card (EPIC), MGNREGA card, or Driving License.
- Active savings bank or post office account with auto-debit facility for premium payment.
